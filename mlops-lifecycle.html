Model Lifecycle Loop

The model lifecycle drawn as what it actually is — a loop, not a line. Data becomes a model, the model is evaluated, gated, deployed and served, monitoring catches drift, and the trigger sends it back around. Two stages are hard gates. Walk the ring, or tap any stage.

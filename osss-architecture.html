Open Sports Scheduling Standard

A neutral, machine-readable language for describing sports scheduling problems and solutions — teams, venues, fixtures, officials, resources, constraints, objectives, and explainable outcomes. It standardizes the problem, not the solver: any compliant engine can consume OSSS inputs, produce OSSS-compliant schedules, and be audited by OSSS rules.

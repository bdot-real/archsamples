Multi-Sensor Fusion

The optional layer that fuses video pose with a wearable IMU using an Extended Kalman Filter — probabilistic state estimation, a deliberately different class of AI from the language models elsewhere in the collection. It sits inside the biomechanics pipeline and is entirely optional: no IMU, and the pipeline runs vision-only.
